# Farm Advisor

[Español](README.es.md)

**Farm Advisor** tells you, right in the tooltip, whether a mob is worth killing in **World of Warcraft: Forever**. See which of its drops you need for your quests and professions, with live drop-rate estimates that improve as you play. Get unified needs lists, recipe suggestions to level faster, farming routes, per-NPC value with Auctionator support, alt tracking and ruleset filters. Hides in combat by default. English and Spanish.

> **Status: planning (v0.1.0).** No addon code yet; this repository only holds the documentation. The roadmap below is the intended scope and may change.

## Planned features

**Phase 1 (MVP)**
- NPC tooltip with the drops you care about: active-quest items, materials for your current professions, estimated drop rate with a confidence indicator, and how many you have and still need.
- Optional: also show what it drops that you could learn later with your current professions.
- Hides in combat (on by default, configurable).
- Personal loot log per NPC (npcID taken from the GUID): kills and drops per item.
- Blended rates: database prior mixed with what you observe, `p = (p0*k + obtained) / (k + kills)`, with a configurable `k` (default 20). Until you have your own data, only the database value is shown.
- Options panel and full English and Spanish localization (client language is detected).

**Phase 2** — unified needs list (quests, chosen recipes, manual goals, camp), reverse lookup of NPCs by needed item (filter by zone and level), recommended levelling recipes and where to farm their materials, progress counters with optional completion alert.

**Phase 3** — per-NPC value (gold/hour or score, optional Auctionator integration), farming routes per zone, all your characters (professions, recipes, needs; "your alt X needs this" in the tooltip), ruleset filters (Normal, PvP, RP, RP-PvP, Hardcore).

**Phase 4** — minimal camp helper (what you need to upgrade your camp) and any learning-ahead features not yet covered.

The addon will have a single main window and one options panel; everything non-essential is optional.

## Requirements

- World of Warcraft: Forever client (modern client, Interface 16001; to be confirmed in game).
- No mandatory dependencies. Auctionator is optional.

## Data

Drop, recipe and quest data will be generated by a separate tool in `tools/` and loaded lazily by the addon. Data without information is shown as "no data", never invented, and every value carries its provenance (database, observed or blended). The terms of use of the data sources are being reviewed before anything is fetched.
