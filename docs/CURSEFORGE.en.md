# Farm Advisor — CurseForge description (EN)

**Is this mob worth killing?** Farm Advisor tells you, right in the tooltip, whether a mob is worth killing in World of Warcraft: Forever. See which of its drops you need for your quests and professions, with live drop-rate estimates that improve as you play.

## Features
- Tooltip with quest items and profession materials the mob drops, how many you have and how many you still need.
- Drop rates that blend the database with what you observe, with a confidence indicator.
- Unified needs lists, recipe suggestions to level faster and farming routes.
- Per-NPC value with optional Auctionator support.
- Alt tracking and ruleset filters (Normal, PvP, RP, RP-PvP, Hardcore).
- Hides in combat by default. English and Spanish.

*Features are rolled out in phases; see the changelog for what each version includes.*
